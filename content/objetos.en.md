### 🔄 Trade evolutions without trading
Use the item on the Pokémon from the bag.

- **Linking Cord:** Kadabra, Machoke, Graveler, Haunter and the other no-item trade evolutions.
- **Metal Coat:** Onix, Scyther.
- **King's Rock:** Poliwhirl, Slowpoke.
- **Dragon Scale:** Seadra.
- **Up-Grade:** Porygon. **Dubious Disc:** Porygon2.
- **Protector:** Rhydon. **Electirizer:** Electabuzz. **Magmarizer:** Magmar.
- **Reaper Cloth:** Dusclops.
- **Deep Sea Tooth / Deep Sea Scale:** Clamperl.

### 🥚 Baby Pokémon
Breed at the Day Care with the parent holding the right incense: Sea, Lax, Rose, Pure, Rock, Odd, Full, Wave or Luck Incense.

### 💪 EV training
The Genesis merchant also sells the **Power items** (Power Weight, Bracer, Belt, Lens, Band and Anklet): each one gives +8 EVs in one stat for every Pokémon you defeat. To undo it, the Pomeg, Kelpsy, Qualot, Hondew, Grepa and Tamato **berries** lower 10 EVs each. Vitamins go all the way to 252.

### 💿 Reusable TMs
Teaching a TM no longer uses it up: teach it to your whole team.
