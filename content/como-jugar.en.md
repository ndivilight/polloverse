You need a Game Boy Advance emulator. Download the ROM and open it from the emulator.

### 💻 PC (Windows, macOS, Linux)
[mGBA](https://mgba.io/downloads.html). Nothing to install: open the program and load the `.gba`.

### 📱 iPhone / iPad
[Delta](https://apps.apple.com/app/delta-game-emulator/id1048524688), free on the App Store. Import the `.gba` from the Files app.

### 🤖 Android
[Pizza Boy GBA](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=it.dbtecno.pizzaboygba) or [mGBA](https://mgba.io/downloads.html).

### 🐾 Follower Pokémon
Your first Pokémon walks behind you. If your emulator gets slow or glitchy (for example on Delta), turn it off in **START → OPTION → FOLLOWER: OFF**.
